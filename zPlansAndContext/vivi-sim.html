What the simulator assumes
  • Goldfish: three opponents at 40 life who never interact. Counterspells and removal sit in hand as dead cards (Lightning Bolt goes face).
  • Mana colours are ignored; every land makes one mana (Ancient Tomb makes two). Signets and Talismans count as +1 mana.
  • Mulligan if the opening seven has fewer than 2 or more than 5 lands. The first mulligan is free; the second bottoms one card.
  • Vivi is cast as soon as 3 mana is free. Its {0} mana ability is used as late in the turn as possible, at its highest power.
  • Curiosity, Ophidian Eye and Tandem Lookout each draw 3 cards whenever Vivi's trigger hits the table.
  • Card selection (Ponder, Brainstorm, Impulse, Stock Up and others) keeps the most useful card: missing combo pieces first, then tutors, lands when short, rocks early.
  • Tutors fire when a combo is one piece away. Mystical Tutor puts the card on top; Gamble discards a random card afterward.
  • Rhystic Study (Stay with Me) draws one card per turn cycle; Mystic Remora draws one per cycle for two cycles. Jeska's Will adds 5 to 6 mana and, with Vivi out, three playable cards.
  • Win checks: Scepter + Reversal (needs 2 mana from rocks with Vivi out, 3 without); Hullbreaker with Sol Ring and another rock; Kiki-Jiki or Splinter Twin with Pestermite, Deceiver Exarch or Zealous Conscripts; Dualcaster + Twinflame or Heat Shimmer; Grapeshot for exact lethal; Vivi pings alone.
  • It's a heuristic pilot, not a perfect player. Use it to compare versions of the deck rather than as an exact win rate.
